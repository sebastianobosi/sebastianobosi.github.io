UCI Cinemas

The redesign of the app for buying tickets, ordering at the bar and getting recommendations from Iris, an AI assistant.

Role
UX/UI Designer · personal project
Platform
Mobile app
Tools
Figma

All work

In short

Problem

Buying a ticket takes too many steps, and quick purchase doesn’t help you choose the film.

My role

Solo project: discovery, brand identity, UX, UI, design system, accessibility and motion.

What I did

A new identity, 85 screens across 9 areas, 80 components and Iris, the AI assistant that guides the purchase.

Outcome

A complete, accessible prototype with a recognisable identity, ready for user testing.

The project

I redesigned the UCI Cinemas app from scratch as a personal project, starting from a new brand identity. The goal: make buying a ticket simpler and faster, and replace quick purchase with Iris, an AI assistant that finds films, showtimes and seats.

Note

Project not commissioned by UCI. Prices, film titles and some data are plausible examples, to be verified. Concept, copy, screens and video are original work by Sebastiano Bosi and are protected by copyright (legal notice).

Process

From discovery to accessibility, with the design system at the centre.

  1. Discovery

    Problem reframed and assumptions mapped before designing a single screen.

  2. Brand and design system

    New identity, tokens and components with variants, states and a changelog.

  3. Flows

    85 screens: main path, errors, empty, offline and loading states for every area.

  4. Accessibility

    Legibility and contrast checked in both themes, with the fixes recorded in the design system.

Brand identity

A new identity built around a single mark: the yellow ellipse. It is the logo, the app symbol, Iris’s button and the gesture that ties every animation together.

Iris, the assistant

In place of quick purchase there is Iris: a minimal yellow ellipse with two square eyes that recall the perforations of film stock. She opens from the Home and guides the purchase as a conversation: film, showtime, seats, summary.

Decision

The user always confirms the payment with Face ID: Iris never pays on her own.

Design system

Every screen uses design system components: first reuse what exists, then create what’s new and add it to the library, with every change in the changelog.

  • Floating bars above the content (Tab Bar, summary, actions), with a fade at the scrolling edge.

  • Consistent shapes at every level, from cards to buttons, for a soft and tidy interface.

  • Essential icons and actions always in the same place: people using the app know where to look.

Accessibility

  • Legible in every theme

    Contrast checked in both the blue and the dark theme, so text and states are always clear.

  • Easy to tap

    Buttons and controls with large touch targets, comfortable even one-handed.

  • For everyone

    Text that scales up, screen reader support and information never conveyed by colour alone.

What I learned

Starting from the design system made the 85 screens faster: every new component went straight into the library, with its changelog.

I would make the same choice again to always have the user confirm the payment: an AI assistant should help you decide, not decide for you.

I would change when I check accessibility: I fixed some contrast issues once the screens were finished. Today I check them as soon as I define the colours.
