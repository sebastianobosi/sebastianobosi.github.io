Hi, I’m Sebastiano.

Before I design anything, I want to understand where people get stuck. I listen to them, write down my doubts and check them with a test. Then I design the screens, including the ones that often get forgotten, like errors or empty pages, and collect them in a design system developers can use without guesswork.

How I work

Four things I do on every project.

  • ResearchI talk to the people who use the product and check my hypotheses with small tests before I open Figma.
  • InterfacesI also design the less pretty cases: what happens when there’s no connection, when a list is empty, when something goes wrong.
  • Design systemEvery component has a name, a few rules and a change log, so design and code stay the same.
  • AccessibilityWhile I design, I check that text is easy to read, buttons are easy to tap and everything works with a screen reader too.
